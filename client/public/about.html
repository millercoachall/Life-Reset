About Life Reset

Life Reset — the household organizer that helps you catch up.

Life Reset is a household organizer that turns the chaos in your head into one calm, shared plan.

What it does

You brain-dump everything you are carrying — the bills, the practices, the permission slips, the things only you seem to remember — and Life Reset sorts it into one plan your whole household can see. Everyone knows what is next, and you always know the first step.

It works in three simple moves:

  1. Capture everything. Get it out of your head in two minutes.
  2. Sort it into one shared plan. Today, this week, who is doing what.
  3. Reset your day. Pick the next step and let the rest wait.

Who builds it

Life Reset is built by Brittany Miller — a mom of eight who spent sixteen years coaching and teaching in higher education, and who knows what it is like to be the one person holding the whole household together. She built the app she needed for her own family, and now shares it with yours.
